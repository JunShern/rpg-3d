[The stable loft of the Hero's Rest. Snow against the one window. The player has Wynn for the first time: she is quicker than Edda and can go under things. Her horns are in a flour sack, because the innkeeper said not indoors.]

[A cat on a roof beam. An older girl in a surcoat too big for her is reaching up at it with a sword, flat side.]

WYNN: You'll hurt it.
IZZY: I'm using the flat! I'm not, I'm trying to get it to step on.
WYNN: Cats don't step on. You have to be boring till they come.

[PROMPT: Sit. Then nothing. A small meter fills for as long as the player presses no button at all.]

[The cat comes down the post, walks past both of them and gets into the flour sack.]

IZZY: How did you do that?
WYNN: We've got eleven. What's your sword called?
IZZY: It isn't yet. They said I'm to name it when I've done something with it.
WYNN: What've you done with it?
IZZY: Cat. Nearly.
IZZY: I'm Izzy. Isolde. Izzy.
WYNN: Wynn. Are you a soldier?
IZZY: I'm the, I'm with Sir Garrick. I'm learning.
WYNN: Learning what?
IZZY: Sword. Mostly sword. And standing. There's a way you stand so that people feel better.
WYNN: Show me.

[Izzy does the Hero's stance. It is, in fact, very good.]

WYNN: I do feel a bit better.
IZZY: Really?
WYNN: I've got one too. Father Penhallow taught me. It's for the opposite.

> Show her.
  [SCOWL, used outside battle. The lamp gutters. A horse below kicks its stall. The cat leaves the sack.]
  IZZY: That's really good.
  WYNN: It used to not do anything.
> 'I'm not meant to do it inside.'
  IZZY: Is it rude?
  WYNN: It makes the milk go.

IZZY: What's in the sack?
WYNN: My horns. I'm not allowed them on indoors.

[Izzy laughs, then sees she means it.]

IZZY: For a pageant?
WYNN: Sort of. It's our turn this year. Our village. I got picked.
WYNN: Did you get picked?
IZZY: Yes. They've a bowl, at the Foundling House.
WYNN: We've got a bowl! It's the pig bowl.
IZZY: Ours is for who washes up.
WYNN: Was your mum cross?
IZZY: I haven't one. That's what a foundling is.
WYNN: Mine's dead. Edda does it. She's cross all the time but it isn't at me.
IZZY: What do you have to do? When you're picked?
WYNN: Go to the keep and sit in the chair and be dreadful till midwinter. Then somebody comes and it's finished.
WYNN: What do you have to do?
IZZY: Go to a keep.
IZZY: At midwinter.

[The cat comes back and sits between them.]

WYNN: Oh.
IZZY: Sir Garrick said it would be big. He said I'd know it when I saw it. He said it wouldn't look like a person.
WYNN: I don't, yet. Father Penhallow says I will by first frost.
WYNN: It's past first frost.
IZZY: You look like a person.
WYNN: Thanks.

> 'Are you any good with it? The sword.'
  IZZY: No.
  WYNN: Good.
  IZZY: I'm getting better, though. I can't help it. Everything we meet on the road, I get better.
  WYNN: Me too.
> 'Does it hurt?'
  IZZY: I don't know. Nobody's said. I'd do it quick.
  IZZY: Sorry. That's a horrible thing to say.
  WYNN: No. Quick's good.

IZZY: We could just not.
WYNN: Not what?
IZZY: Any of it. I don't go up, you don't be dreadful. We both just don't.
WYNN: Then the harvest doesn't get blessed.
IZZY: Does it, though? Really?
WYNN: Netherby's is ever so good.

[A voice from the yard. EDDA: 'Wynn! Where've you got to?']

WYNN: I have to go. Don't tell them. They'll make it into a thing.
IZZY: I won't.
WYNN: You can have the cat. It isn't mine.

[Wynn takes the flour sack and goes down the ladder. The player keeps Izzy for a moment, alone in the loft. Her status page has changed. Weapon: 'Cat'.]
