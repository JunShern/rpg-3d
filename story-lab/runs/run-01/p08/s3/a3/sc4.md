[The common room, past midnight. The fire is low. Sir Garrick Lowe has the settle and a jug. Edda has come down for water. The stairs behind her stay lit: the player can walk back up them at any point and the scene ends there.]

GARRICK: You're the sister.
EDDA: I'm getting water.
GARRICK: Bucket's by the door. It's froze. Sit while it thaws. I'll not bite, I'm retired.
EDDA: I know who you are. There's a plate of you in our church.
GARRICK: Is it the one with the foot on its neck?
EDDA: Yes.
GARRICK: I never did that. The engraver added the foot.

[Edda sits. Not near.]

GARRICK: She's small. Yours.
EDDA: She's twelve.
GARRICK: Mine was forty-odd. Aldric Coombe, out of Netherby. Drank. Hit his wife.
GARRICK: When the lot came up on him the whole parish said, well, there you are. Providence knows its business.
GARRICK: I thought so too. I was twenty-five and I thought, good. A bad one.
EDDA: The lot's the lot.
GARRICK: Is it. I never asked, in my day.

> 'What happens at the end? Tell me properly.'
  GARRICK: Properly. Nobody's asked me properly in thirty years. They ask for the story.
> 'I don't want to hear this.'
  GARRICK: No. Nor did I. The stairs are there.
  [The stairs are there. If the player stays in the room, he goes on.]

GARRICK: You go up through the keep. It's all his by then. The rooms are how he thinks.
GARRICK: Coombe's was a taproom. Every floor, the same taproom, with the stools too high. And things in it that had been people's dogs.
GARRICK: At the top there's what the pictures show. Big. Horns that are its own. A voice like a mill.
GARRICK: I fought that for an hour and I was glad to. That's the part they engrave.
GARRICK: Then it goes down. And when it's down, it goes small. Nobody tells you that. It goes back.
GARRICK: And there's a man from Netherby on the floor in his shirt, and he looks up and says, 'Is it done? Can I go home?'

[The bucket ticks as the ice lets go.]

GARRICK: And the compact's not kept till it's finished.
EDDA: Father Penhallow says it is the Office that dies.
GARRICK: Aye. It does. It dies last.
EDDA: Why are you telling me?
GARRICK: Because yours is twelve. And mine is sixteen and says her prayers, and I've to teach her how.
GARRICK: I thought somebody on your side should know what I'm teaching.
EDDA: I haven't got a side. I'm taking her where I've been told. If we do it all right...
GARRICK: I did it all right. Ask anyone. There's a plate.

> 'Did the harvests come?'
  GARRICK: Thirty years. The best anyone has known.
  GARRICK: That's the worst of it, lass. It works.
> 'What did you say to him? Coombe.'
  GARRICK: I said, 'Nearly.'
  GARRICK: God forgive me. I said, 'Nearly, Aldric.'

GARRICK: Your water's thawed.

[Edda takes the bucket. At the foot of the stairs she stops.]

EDDA: She's getting stronger. Your girl. I saw her in the yard.
GARRICK: So is yours.
EDDA: I know. I can't stop it. Every wolf on the road, she...
GARRICK: Then stop killing wolves.
EDDA: They come at us.
GARRICK: They come at you. Have you watched what they do when they get to her?

[Edda goes up. Wynn's status page has a new line the player can read: 'Things that will not harm her: 14.']
